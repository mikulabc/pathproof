# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Protect the privacy and security of users and customers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

This policy applies in all project spaces and whenever someone represents the project publicly.
